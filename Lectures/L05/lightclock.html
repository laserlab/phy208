Both photons do c. That is the second postulate and it is not negotiable. The zigzag is longer than the straight bounce, so the moving clock completes fewer round trips — yours ÷ hers → γ.

In her frame her photon bounces straight up and down and it is your clock that zigzags. Neither clock is broken; "slow" is a relation between frames, not a property of a clock.
