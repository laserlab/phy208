Nothing here is invented. The sea-level muon flux is about 1 per cm² per minute; arrivals are Poisson — random, memoryless, no schedule. The clicks you hear are the statistics of the sky.

Same rain, two windows: the laptop-lid patch ticks lazily; the full square meter turns it into a downpour — ~170 muons every second.

Your hand: ≈ 70 cm² → about 70 muons/min — one per second, through your palm, since before you were born.
