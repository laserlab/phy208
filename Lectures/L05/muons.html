Same muons. Each pair shares one random proper lifetime drawn from the 2.2 μs exponential. The only difference between the two skies is whether the 15 km trip is charged against τ — or against γτ.

Slide γ down to 1 and the two skies agree — and go dark. The measured sea-level rain (~1 muon/cm²/min) is the right-hand column.
