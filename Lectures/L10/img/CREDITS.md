## Figures generated from code

The two-worldline reunion sketch, the one-frame/two-frame comparison, the
four-route proper-time figure, the outbound/inbound simultaneity panels, the
Hafele–Keating bar chart, and every computed table (the trip arithmetic, the
distance-scaling cell, the finite-turnaround integration, the Doppler count,
and the three worked activity rows) are generated by the Python cells in the
lecture source. Original work, CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Data cited in figures

The Hafele–Keating values (predicted −59 / measured −51 ± 10 ns eastward;
predicted 273 / measured 280 ± 21 ns westward) are the published numbers from
*Science* **177** 166 and 168 (1972), reproduced as data, not as a figure.
