**Both world maps are drawn from one dataset**, Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0
countries (`tools/ne_110m_countries.geojson`), which is **public domain** — no
attribution required, though the project asks to be credited. Rendering them
from the same outlines is deliberate: two maps pulled from different sources
would not be a fair comparison, which is the point the slide makes.

The Equal Earth projection is by Šavrič, Patterson & Jenny (2018), implemented
from the published polynomial in `make_map_figs.py`. A projection is a formula,
not a copyrightable work. Mercator's is from 1569 and long out of copyright.

The UN General Assembly adopted resolution *Correct the Map* on 4 September
2026, 164–1–6, urging equal-area projections and naming Equal Earth. Cited on
the slide as fact, no UN artwork used.
