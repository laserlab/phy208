## Figures generated from code

The two-frame clay collision, the momentum curve (Newton's $mv$ against
$\gamma mv$), the energy curve with the rest-energy floor, the momenergy
diagram with the mass hyperbola and three arrows, and the photon-versus-massive
momenergy sketch are generated by the Python cells in the lecture source.
Original work, CC BY-SA 4.0.

The velocity-addition numbers on the collision slide (0.882c for the left
lump seen from the right lump, and the Newtonian sums −0.88m and −1.20m) are
computed in the cell, not typed.
